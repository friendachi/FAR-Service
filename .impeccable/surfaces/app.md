OWN-WORLD: DOH sign green #00703C ground with white type and a white inner border keyline on sign panels; deep green #003B20 for shell/text-on-light; reflective yellow #FFD200 reserved for primary action and attention; white content ground. Yards are route shields (1 บางพระ, 2 หนองใหญ่) with numbered shield badges; directional arrows mark next step. Anuphan type, bold sign-weight headings. Flat colour, no gradients, no soft shadows.
STORY: Visitor sees two yards as destinations, picks type/yard/dates, sees rate tier + deposit before committing; staff and drivers see the same sign language as status and wayfinding.
FIRST VIEWPORT: green top bar; full-width sign panel (keyline border, rounded) with headline left and yard shields with arrows right; search row of four fields + yellow "ค้นหาหางว่าง"; trailer cards beneath.
